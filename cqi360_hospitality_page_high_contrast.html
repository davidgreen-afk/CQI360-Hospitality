The challenge

Busy doesn't always mean effective.

A business can be popular and still lose ground to uneven execution, preventable friction, unclear accountability, and constant firefighting. We focus on the operating conditions that make good hospitality repeatable.

01 / PEOPLE

Teams & leadership

Role clarity, onboarding, supervisor effectiveness, communication, development, and team consistency.

02 / OPERATIONS

Systems & service

Workflows, handoffs, service standards, recurring breakdowns, and the gap between the intended and actual guest experience.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Measures & decisions

Useful KPIs, feedback loops, management rhythms, priorities, and evidence of whether changes are helping.

How CQI360 works

From observation to measurable improvement.

Every engagement starts with the realities of your operation. Rather than imposing a generic playbook, we work with owners and leaders to understand the problem, test assumptions, and make changes teams can sustain.

1

Study

Listen, observe, review available evidence, and clarify where friction appears.

2

Design

Develop focused improvements to processes, management practices, training, or measurement.

3

Evaluate

Track a small set of meaningful indicators and learn what actually changes.

4

Scale

Standardize what works and help teams apply it consistently across shifts or locations.

Examples of where we can help

  • Service consistency and operational handoffs
  • Manager and supervisor development
  • Onboarding, training, and team readiness
  • Leadership dashboards and meaningful KPIs
  • Growth-readiness and multi-location alignment
  • Resource stewardship and sustainability-related decisions

Engagements are tailored to the operation and its goals; we do not substitute for specialist culinary, accounting, or hospitality regulatory services.
